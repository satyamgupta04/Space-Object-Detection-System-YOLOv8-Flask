🚀 SpaceSafe-AI

In space stations, quick access to critical equipment like Fire Extinguishers, Oxygen Tanks, and Toolboxes is vital for astronaut safety and mission success. Manual monitoring is difficult due to the complex and confined environment of a space station. Our AI system uses YOLOv8 to automatically detect and highlight essential equipment from images, ensuring safety and operational efficiency.
